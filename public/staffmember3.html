FAQ

Quick answers for busy parents.

How can I enroll my child?

You can enroll by filling out the application form on our website or contacting our office directly.

Do you offer lunch to every kid?

Yes, we provide a nutritious lunch to every child enrolled in our program

Do you offer after-school programs?

Yes, we provide a variety of after-school enrichment activities, including ICT skills.

What age group is Kids First for?

KidsFirst programs are designed for children aged 2 and above.
